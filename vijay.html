C. Joseph Vijay, known as Thalapathy Vijay, is an Indian politician and former Tamil cinema superstar, serving as the 9th Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu since May 2026 and founder of the Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK). With 69 films from Naalaiya Theerpu (1992) to Jana Nayagan (2026), he built a career mixing romance, action, and social commentary in hits like Ghilli, Thuppakki, Mersal, and Leo. His fan network evolved into the Vijay Makkal Iyakkam, a key grassroots base for TVK’s electoral breakthrough, winning 108 seats in its debut. Acclaimed for his philanthropy, he has received multiple Tamil Nadu State Film Awards, the Kalaimaamani award, and an honorary doctorate.
